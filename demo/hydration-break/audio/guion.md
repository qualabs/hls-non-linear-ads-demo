## El prompt de estilo de cada voz

**Charon, el relato:**

> A calm British football commentator on live television, during a lull in the match. Warm, level, unhurried. Never shouting, never like an advertisement.

**Kore, el comentario:**

> A calm British co-commentator, a former player, answering her colleague on live television. Warm, level, unhurried, conversational. Never shouting, never like an advertisement.

## Los prompts de las tres camas

**KALTO:**

> Fast, hard-driving instrumental electronic music at one hundred and forty beats per minute, urgent and athletic, the kind of track played over a sprint. A relentless four-on-the-floor kick drum on every beat, a hard driving sixteenth-note synthesiser bassline, a bright aggressive lead riff and a sharp snare on two and four. High energy from the very first beat, never relaxed, never mellow, never slow, with no build-up and no quiet introduction. Purely instrumental, with no voice, no singing and no speech of any kind.

**MERIDIA:**

> An instrumental music bed for a television advertisement about a seaside holiday, twenty-four seconds long. Open, airy and unhurried: a slow warm pad underneath, a gentle nylon-string guitar figure, soft mallet notes and a light shaker keeping an easy pulse around ninety beats per minute. It feels like open water and distance, calm and inviting, and it keeps the same mood and the same density from the first second to the last. Purely instrumental, with no voice, no singing and no speech of any kind.

**NEONECTAR:**

> Eight seconds of bright, punchy instrumental music for a soft-drink television advertisement. Fizzing and electric: a snappy beat around one hundred and twenty beats per minute, a bouncing synthesiser bass, sparkling bell-like plucks and a wide shimmering chord that opens out. Fresh, sweet and upbeat, energetic from the very first beat with no quiet introduction. Purely instrumental, with no voice, no singing and no speech of any kind.
