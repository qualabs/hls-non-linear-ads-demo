# Credits

**Everything on screen and everything you hear was generated for this demo. There is no
third-party material in it and therefore no licence to honour.** That is worth saying out
loud rather than leaving a reader to assume it: the other demos in this repository run on
the Blender Foundation's open movies and credit them file by file, and a page that said
nothing would read like one of those with its credits missing.

Nothing was filmed, nothing was licensed and nothing was bought. The race, the circuit, the
cars, the crowd, the engines and the two commentators do not exist.

| Piece | What it is | How it was produced | Model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The programme | the race coverage, cutting between cars the way a director cuts | fourteen independent clips of eight seconds, generated one per shot from the plan of shots and concatenated flat by `scripts/armar-programa.sh`, with the ambience of each clip levelled clip by clip | `veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`, 720p, 24 fps, **with audio** |
| Each camera of the catalogue | an on-board or trackside feed of one car, lasting exactly as long as the window | eight clips of eight seconds per camera, generated by `scripts/generar-camara.py` and `scripts/generar-camaras.py`, and concatenated by `scripts/armar-camara.sh`, levelled to the same loudness as the programme so that enlarging a box is a change of content and not a jump in volume | `veo-3.1-fast-generate-001`, 720p, 24 fps, **with audio** |
| The commentary | two commentators, one line per audio file, placed over the programme by `scripts/armar-relato.mjs` | synthesised line by line by `scripts/generar-relato.sh` in en-GB, two voices cast by function — Charon calls what is happening, Kore says why it matters — each line transcribed and gated before it is kept | `gemini-2.5-flash-tts`; the gate that listens to each line back is `gemini-2.5-flash` |
| The cars | the six liveries, invented so that no real team's trade dress appears anywhere | six reference sheets drawn before any video was generated, used to write the colour of each car into every prompt. They are not on screen: they are what made the six cars the same six cars across every independent generation of this race | `generate_image` of the Antigravity CLI (`agy`) |

**The prompt that produced each piece travels with the generator that sent it**, which is
where this project keeps a recipe (ADR 0061): `scripts/generar-programa.py` carries the
fourteen shot prompts, the camera generators the eight of each camera, and
`scripts/relato/guion.json` the lines the commentators say. The reasoning behind the shape of
those prompts — and what was regenerated and why — is in the tasks of phase 13 of the
project, not here.

**The liveries are invented on purpose and it is checked, not hoped for.** Every image and
video prompt carries the same paragraph forbidding the trade dress of any real racing team,
by naming the alternative rather than only the prohibition, and the six colours were chosen
outside the identity of the real teams. Every clip was looked at.

hls.js is vendored unmodified in `vendor/` of the repository, under its own licence
(`vendor/hls.js.LICENSE`).
